Guide

Create & edit

Choose Idea or Note, then click blank canvas to place a blank card. Double-click blank canvas to add a node. Double-click a card to edit it; drag to move it.

Connect

Choose Connect and click two cards, or use a card’s + button. After choosing a source, click blank canvas to create a connected node. Select a line to change its shape or arrows; drag a curve’s handle to bend it. Node links are solid; note links always use dashed arrows. Double-click a line to add or edit a note; clear the text to remove it.

View & appearance

Drag blank canvas, or hold Space and drag, to pan. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Fit to view frames the map. Appearance changes the theme; node options change color. Nodes and notes both offer centered or ragged-right text; note text stays centered within the paper. Ragged-right cards fit their text with equal side padding.

Align nearby

The button beside the header lock aligns nearby rows and columns and equalizes their gaps. Spacing needs at least three cards; outer cards and locked cards anchor each section. Align nearby in a card’s options aligns it and balances its gaps between two aligned neighbours. Locked cards stay fixed. Undo reverses the whole action.

Lock

The header lock freezes existing items while allowing new ones. Click it again to unlock all. Individual items can be locked in their options. Locked cards can still be connected.

Save

Autosave keeps your map in this browser on this device. Export → Editable map (JSON) makes a backup for Import; Vector image (SVG) exports an image. Saved maps aren’t written into the HTML file.
